## Executive Summary & Core Rules

### 1. The Core Principle
> **"Build the business operating system first. Connect the outside world later."**

### 2. The Core Problem
> *"I run a business. I need one simple place to manage my business and understand how it is performing."*

### 3. The Unchanging Foundation
$$\text{Business Management} + \text{Customer Management} + \text{Operations} + \text{Business Performance}$$

### 4. Dual Sides of GetListed
1. **GetListed Business:** Used by owners and staff to manage catalog, bookings, customers, transactions, expenses, team, and reports.
2. **GetListed Customer:** Simple customer portal to discover local businesses, view live availability, book, and track bookings without encountering business complexity.

### 5. Phase 1 vs. Phase 2 Scope Boundaries
* **Phase 1 (Active):** Standalone core operating system. No third-party API dependencies. Native Excel/CSV/PDF exports.
* **Phase 2 (Sequenced):** Connected Business OS (Google APIs, Payment Gateways, WhatsApp Cloud API, Zoho, Tally, E-commerce connectors).
* **Future:** Optimization & recommendations based on real accumulated business data.
