## 6. Business Growth Intelligence

GetListed provides an intentionally lightweight intelligence layer designed to answer simple, high-value questions for the owner:

### Growth Questions
* Is the business growing?
* Is revenue increasing?
* Are bookings increasing?
* Are customers increasing?

### Customer Behaviour Questions
* Are customers returning?
* Which services bring repeat customers?
* How many new customers came this month?

### Business Performance Questions
* Which service performs best?
* Which day is busiest?
* Which month performs best?
* Which periods are slow or weak?

### Actionable Plain-Language Insights
Rather than ambiguous numbers, GetListed explains trends directly:
* *"Your bookings increased 22%, but cancellations also increased 11%."*
* *"Saturday generates the highest number of bookings."*
* *"Your revenue is growing, but average transaction value has decreased."*

> This lightweight interpretation becomes the foundation for the future Phase 2 optimization layer.

## 7. Basic Financial Tracking

GetListed is **NOT** initially a full-fledged accounting suite.  
Phase 1 focuses on clear, manual and semi-automated business financial tracking.

### Income Tracking
The business can record:
* Sales revenue
* Booking revenue
* Service revenue
* Product revenue
* Other miscellaneous income

### Expense Tracking
The business can easily record expenses with categories:
* Rent
* Staff salaries
* Electricity & Utilities
* Internet & Phone
* Marketing & Ads
* Equipment & Maintenance
* Other operating expenses

### Financial Summary
GetListed aggregates these inputs to show:
1. **Total Income**
2. **Total Expenses**
3. **Net Business Amount** (Income minus Expenses)
4. **Period Comparisons** (Week-over-Week, Month-over-Month)
5. **Basic Income/Expense breakdown**

### Important Limitation (Phase 1)
* Phase 1 does not connect to external banking or accounting APIs.
* Financial reporting is strictly derived from **data entered into GetListed**.
* Automated bank and gateway reconciliation is reserved for Phase 2.

## 8. Basic Audit / Accountant-Ready Reports

GetListed does not initially serve as an auditing or tax-filing application.  
Instead, it produces structured business records that can be immediately handed to an accountant, Chartered Accountant (CA), or used for internal review.

### Available Standard Records
* Sales Report
* Expense Report
* Income Report
* Booking Report
* Customer Report
* Payment & Transaction Report
* Monthly Consolidated Transaction Report
* Service-wise Revenue Report
* Product-wise Revenue Report

### Export Formats
* **Excel (.xlsx / .csv)**
* **CSV**
* **PDF**

## 9. Excel / Data Export

> **Core Principle: Excel is treated as a native business utility, not an external integration.**

Local businesses run on spreadsheets. GetListed embraces this natively without requiring external cloud accounts or connectors.

### Standard Exports
GetListed provides clean, pre-formatted standard downloads:
* Customers directory (`Customer Name | Phone | Email | Total Bookings | Total Spent | Last Visit`)
* Bookings history
* Transactions log
* Expenses breakdown
* Services & products catalog
* Revenue & tax-ready summaries

### Custom Exports
Businesses can select:
* Specific columns
* Date ranges
* Status filters
* Sort ordering

This provides immediate real-world utility without forcing complex custom reporting tools.
